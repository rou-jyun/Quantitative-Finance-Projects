# Quantitative Finance Projects

This repository showcases selected quantitative finance projects completed during my undergraduate studies in Mathematics.

My projects focus on applying mathematical and statistical models to financial problems, with an emphasis on quantitative modeling, empirical analysis, and Python implementation.

## Featured Projects

### 01. Heston vs. GARCH(1,1) Models for Option Pricing

A comparative study of two volatility models for S&P 500 option pricing under COVID-19 market conditions.

**Methods & Topics**
- Heston stochastic volatility model
- GARCH(1,1) model
- Model calibration
- In-sample / out-of-sample evaluation
- RMSE
- Monte Carlo simulation

**Focus:** Evaluating the pricing performance and stability of different volatility models under a highly volatile market environment.

---

### 02. ETF Portfolio Allocation

A quantitative portfolio allocation project focusing on long-term capital preservation, downside risk control, and stable returns.

**Methods & Topics**
- Strategic asset allocation
- Stock-bond portfolio construction
- Moving-average signals
- Portfolio rebalancing
- Historical backtesting
- Risk-adjusted performance evaluation

**Focus:** Examining the trade-off between portfolio return and downside risk across different asset allocation strategies.
